excerpt: 'Illinois, like the rest of the country, faces a housing crisis. A major cause of high housing prices is a lack of supply, and while most housing policy focuses on increasing the supply of market-rate housing, a growing voice of housing advocates is calling for new forms of housing ownership. Shared-Equity Housing (SEH) models, like Community Land Trusts and Limited Equity Cooperatives, are forms of owner-occupied housing designed to remain affordable in perpetuity. Despite thier proven ability to create affordable housing, SEH projects in Illinois lack a sufficient, affordable, and accessible funding source to expand their housing operations. This paper explores the current funding environment for SEH in Illinois and discusses three potential solutions that address this financing problem. '
